Templates

The slide patterns in a brand kit. Click a deck to start one, or a slide to start from just that pattern. You can also drop any of these into an open deck from the inspector, or save a slide you've made back into its kit.

Brand kits

A kit is a brand's palette, fonts, logo, writing voice and slide templates. Every deck is generated and rendered through one. Edit a kit and every deck using it follows.

Build a kit from your decks

Upload one or more decks you've already made: a PDF export works best, slide screenshots work, and a .pptx gives the exact theme colours and fonts. Claude reads them and builds the palette, fonts, voice and one template per slide pattern. Takes a minute or two.
